Frequently asked

Is my data really only on my phone?

Yes. Tend has no account system and no server. Everything you log is stored in the app's own local database on your device. See the privacy policy for the full detail.

Do I need Apple Health to use Tend?

No. Health access is entirely optional and can be turned on or off per data type in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health at any time. Everything in Tend works without it — Health just saves you from re-logging things your phone or watch already knows.

Why don't I see any insights yet?

Tend waits until there's enough history — roughly ten days of logging on each side of a comparison — before it will show a pattern. This is intentional: a pattern from three days of data usually isn't a real pattern. Keep checking in, and Insights fills in.

Can I track alcohol, cannabis, or nicotine?

Yes — they're in the item library alongside sleep, caffeine, and exercise, tracked the same neutral way. Tend doesn't judge what you log; it just looks for patterns in how you feel afterward.

Is Tend a substitute for therapy or medical care?

No. Tend is a wellness tool that helps you notice patterns in your own life — it can't diagnose or treat anything, and it never suggests changing a prescribed medication. If you're concerned about your mental health, please talk with a doctor or therapist, and see the crisis resources above if you need support right now.

How do I back up or move my data to a new phone?

Settings → Your data lets you create a full backup file and restore from one — useful for moving to a new device or just keeping your own copy. A CSV export is also available there for opening your data in a spreadsheet.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Your data → Delete all data removes everything Tend has stored, permanently. There's no server copy to also delete — this is the whole thing.

Does Tend show ads or sell my data?

No, on both counts. Tend has no ad SDK, no analytics, and nothing to sell — see the privacy policy for specifics.
