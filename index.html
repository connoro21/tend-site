Notice what tends to help.

Tend is a private daily checklist for the things that shape how you feel — sleep, movement, caffeine, sunlight, people — paired with a quick mood check-in. Over time with Tend Plus it shows you the patterns, in plain language, on your phone only. Logging and check-ins are free.

What it does

A quick daily check-in

Mood, energy, calm, and focus — a few taps, usually ten seconds, any time you open the app.

A checklist you control

Pick from 130+ items across movement, sleep, substances, mind, social, and more. Start small, add as you go.

Patterns, honestly gated

Tend only shows a pattern once there's enough history behind it — no dramatic claims from three days of data.

Apple Health, optionally

Reads steps, sleep, workouts, and more so you don't have to re-log what your phone already knows. Fully optional.

Home Screen & Siri

Log your most-checked items from a widget, or just ask Siri — no need to open the app.

Locked down, your way

Face ID app lock, and a full backup/restore file you control. Tend keeps your data on your iPhone and has no server. Your iPhone's own backup and any file you export are outside Tend's control.

Why it's private by default

  • There's no account and no login — nothing to sign up for.
  • All data lives in the app's own storage on your device.
  • No analytics, no ad tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • HealthKit reads and writes happen on-device, between Tend and Health, and go nowhere else.
  • Tend has no server and never receives your data. Data moves only through Apple's StoreKit (for Tend Plus), links you choose to open, like this page or the 988 crisis line, and your own iOS or iCloud backups.
