Privacy policy

Your data stays on your phone.

Last updated September 25, 2026 · Applies to Tend for iOS, version 1.0

The short version

  • Tend has no account system, no server, and no analytics. Everything you log is stored only in the app's own storage on your iPhone.
  • Apple Health access (if you turn it on) is read and written directly between Tend and the Health app, on your device. It never passes through us.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your data with anyone, because we never receive it in the first place.
  • You can export or delete everything from Settings at any time.

What Tend stores, and where

Tend stores the items you choose to track, the entries you log against them, your mood/energy/calm/focus check-ins, any notes you add, your app settings (theme, reminder time, Face ID lock), and — if you use it — your own backup files. All of it lives in the app's local database on your device, inside a secure App Group container shared only between the Tend app and its Home Screen widget, both of which run on your phone.

Nothing here is uploaded to a server, because Tend doesn't have one. There is no Tend account, no sign-in, and no cloud sync in version 1.0. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it — unless you've saved a backup file yourself.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, Tend can read the following from Apple Health, so you don't have to log what your phone or watch already knows: steps, workouts, sleep, time in daylight, mindful minutes, water intake, menstrual cycle tracking, heart rate variability, and caffeine. Each category is a separate permission you grant (or decline) in Apple's own permission sheet, and you can change any of them later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

With separate permission, Tend can also write your mood check-ins back to Apple Health as State of Mind entries, so they show up alongside your other health data if you choose.

All of this happens on your device. Reading from Health and writing to Health both happen directly between Tend and the Health app on your iPhone — this data is never transmitted to us, to any analytics service, or to any third party. We don't use Health data for advertising, and we never write anything to Health that you didn't enter yourself.

What Tend does not do

No accountsThere's nothing to sign up for and no password to manage.
No analyticsTend doesn't include any analytics or usage-tracking SDK.
No adsTend has no ad SDK and doesn't use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA).
No selling dataWe can't sell what we never receive.
No third-party sharingYour logs, check-ins, and Health data stay between you and your phone.

The only outbound network requests Tend ever makes are links you tap yourself — for example, the 988 crisis line, findahelpline.com, or this website.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Tend schedules local notifications on your device using Apple's own notification system. No notification content or delivery data is sent to us — the schedule lives entirely on your phone.

Export, backup, and deletion

From Settings, you can export your data as a CSV file for a spreadsheet, create a full backup file of your history to restore later or move to a new device, or permanently delete everything Tend has stored. Private items you've marked as private (for example, an intimacy entry) are excluded from exports by default. None of these actions involve us — they happen entirely on your device, to a file you control.

Children

Tend is not directed at children and is not designed for users under 13. It contains mental-health-adjacent content, mood check-ins, and references to substances (as trackable items, not promotion), and is rated for age 12+ on the App Store. Since Tend collects no data from us in the first place, there's no server-side data to worry about — but if you believe a child has used the app and you'd like the on-device data removed, deleting the app removes it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, for any meaningful change, note it in the app's release notes.
