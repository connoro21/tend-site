Privacy policy

Tend never receives your data.

Last updated October 9, 2026 · Applies to Tend for iOS, version 1.0

The short version

  • Tend has no account system, no server, and no analytics. Everything you log is stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone. Tend never receives it.
  • Apple Health access (if you turn it on) is read and written directly between Tend and the Health app, on your device. It never passes through us.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your data with anyone, because we never receive it in the first place.
  • You can export or delete everything from Tend's You tab, under Your data, at any time.

What Tend stores, and where

Tend stores the items you choose to track, the entries you log against them, your mood/energy/calm/focus check-ins, any notes you add, your app settings (theme, reminder time, Face ID lock), and — if you use it — your own backup files. All of it lives in the app's local database on your device, inside a secure App Group container shared only between the Tend app and its Home Screen widget, both of which run on your phone.

Nothing here is uploaded to a server, because Tend doesn't have one. There is no Tend account, no sign-in, and no cloud sync in version 1.0. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it, unless you've saved a backup file yourself or your iPhone backup holds a copy.

iPhone backups. Tend's data is not excluded from your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup, so that backup may include it, under your Apple Account's terms. Tend doesn't control that backup.

Face ID. The optional app lock uses Apple's Face ID and passcode system. Tend never receives your biometric data. Reminder notifications use general wording and don't include your logs. Widgets and the lock-screen progress widget hide your data while the app lock is on; what else appears on your Lock Screen follows your iPhone's Lock Screen settings.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, Tend can read the following from Apple Health, so you don't have to log what your phone or watch already knows: steps, workouts, sleep, time in daylight, mindful minutes, water intake, menstrual cycle tracking, heart rate variability, and caffeine. Each category is a separate permission you grant (or decline) in Apple's own permission sheet, and you can change any of them later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

With separate permission, Tend can also write your mood check-ins back to Apple Health as State of Mind entries, so they show up alongside your other health data if you choose.

All of this happens on your device. Reading from Health and writing to Health both happen directly between Tend and the Health app on your iPhone — this data is never transmitted to us, to any analytics service, or to any third party. We don't use Health data for advertising, and we never write anything to Health that you didn't enter yourself.

Consumer health data

This section is Tend's Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy, for the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada SB 370 and similar laws. It applies to everyone, wherever you live.

Categories. The health and wellness data you enter in Tend or choose to import from Apple Health: mood, energy, calm and focus check-ins; notes; medication and supplement logs; menstrual flow and birth control entries; intimacy entries; sleep, steps, workouts, heart rate variability, caffeine, water, mindful minutes and time in daylight.

Purpose. Only to show you your own logs and patterns on your device.

Collection, sale and sharing. The developer does not collect this data: it stays on your iPhone and Tend has no server. It is never sold and never shared with any third party, processor or affiliate. The only ways it moves are your own iOS or iCloud backups and files you export and share yourself.

Withdraw consent. Turn off any Apple Health category, or Tend's access to Health entirely, in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health. Stop logging at any time, or delete entries or items inside Tend.

Deletion. In Tend, You → Your data → Erase everything deletes it all from your device (see Export, backup, and deletion above for the Apple Health and backup details). Because the developer holds no copy, there is nothing further to delete on our side.

Tend has no server of its own and never receives your data. Data moves only through Apple's StoreKit for Tend Plus (see Purchases below), links you choose to open (for example the 988 crisis line, findahelpline.com, or this website), and your own iOS or iCloud backups.

Do Not Track. Tend does not track you across sites or apps, so there is nothing to opt out of.

This website. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses under its own privacy statement. We run no analytics or cookies on this site, and its fonts are served from the site itself.

Purchases

Tend Plus is sold and billed by Apple. When you buy it, restore it or manage it, you deal with Apple's own App Store screens, and Apple handles payment.

To know whether Tend Plus is on, Tend asks Apple's StoreKit on your iPhone whether your Apple Account has an active subscription. StoreKit may contact Apple to load the plans and prices, check your subscription or restore a purchase, and may ask you to sign in to your Apple Account. That traffic is between your iPhone and Apple. Tend never sees your payment details or your Apple Account, and it sends nothing about your purchases anywhere: there is no Tend server.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Tend schedules local notifications on your device using Apple's own notification system. No notification content or delivery data is sent to us — the schedule lives entirely on your phone.

Export, backup, and deletion

From Tend's You tab, under Your data, you can export your data as a CSV file for a spreadsheet, create a full backup file of your history to restore later or move to a new device, or permanently delete everything Tend has stored. None of these actions involve us — they happen entirely on your device, to a file you control.

The CSV export leaves out items you've marked private (for example, an intimacy entry) unless you choose to include them. The backup file is a full plaintext copy of your history, private items included, and is not encrypted. Where it goes after you tap Share (Files, Mail, another app) is your choice and outside Tend's control; keep it somewhere safe.

Erase everything permanently deletes what Tend has stored on your device. It also removes the State of Mind entries Tend saved to Apple Health in the last 30 days; older entries stay in Apple Health, and you delete those in the Health app. Erasing can't be undone, and it doesn't touch copies in your iPhone backups or backup files you've saved.

Children

Tend is rated 13+ on the App Store and is not directed to children under 13. It contains mental-health-adjacent content, mood check-ins, and references to substances (as trackable items, not promotion). Tend collects no information from anyone, including children, because it has no server. If a parent wants a child's on-device data removed, deleting the app or using You → Your data → Erase everything in Tend removes it.
